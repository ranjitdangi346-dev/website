Non-Destructive Testing
NDT SERVICES
Precision non-destructive testing for industrial equipment, structures, welds, and amusement rides — detecting defects without causing damage.
UT
Ultrasonic Testing
High-frequency sound waves detect internal defects and measure material thickness without damaging the component. Used for pressure vessels, pipelines, welds, and ride structures.
DPT
Dye Penetrant Testing
Surface cracks and discontinuities are identified using dye penetration techniques. Highly effective for detecting surface-breaking defects in all non-porous materials.
MPT
Magnetic Particle Testing
Detects surface and near-surface cracks in ferromagnetic materials. Critical for weld inspection and structural integrity verification of steel components.
VT
Visual Testing
Systematic visual inspection of surfaces, welds, and components to identify visible defects, corrosion, and dimensional non-conformances.
TM
Thickness Measurement
Ultrasonic thickness gauging for corrosion monitoring, remaining wall thickness assessment, and fitness-for-service evaluations.
